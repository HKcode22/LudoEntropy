# Ludo Game - Gameplay Guide

## How to Play

### Starting the Game

1. **Quick Play** (Recommended for testing)
   - Click "Quick Play" button on the main menu
   - Game starts immediately with 4 players (you + 3 AI opponents)
   - You play as RED

2. **Create Game** (For multiplayer)
   - Click "Create Game" to get a room code
   - Share the 6-digit code with friends
   - Wait for players to join
   - Click "Start Game" when ready

3. **Join Game**
   - Click "Join Game"
   - Enter the 6-digit room code
   - Wait in lobby for host to start

### Game Controls

#### Rolling the Dice
- Click the **"Roll Dice"** button OR
- Click directly on the **dice** itself
- The dice will animate and show a random number (1-6)

#### Moving Pieces
1. After rolling, **movable pieces will be highlighted with a golden ring**
2. **Click on any highlighted piece** to move it
3. The piece will automatically move by the dice value

### Game Rules

#### Bringing Pieces Out
- All 4 pieces start in your home base (colored corner)
- You need to roll a **6** to bring a piece out onto the board
- When you roll a 6, click on any piece in your home base

#### Moving Pieces
- After a piece is on the board, move it by the dice value
- Pieces move clockwise around the board
- Then enter your colored home path (the colored column/row leading to center)

#### Capturing Opponents
- Landing on an opponent's piece sends them back to their base
- **Safe spots (stars)** protect pieces from capture
- Multiple pieces can share safe spots

#### Winning
- Get all 4 pieces to the center triangle
- You need an **exact roll** to enter the final home position
- First player to get all 4 pieces home wins!

#### Extra Turns
- Rolling a **6** gives you another turn
- Bringing a piece out with a 6 also gives another turn
- Capturing an opponent gives another turn

### Visual Indicators

- **Golden ring around pieces** = Can be moved
- **Green ring** = Currently selected piece
- **Cursor changes to pointer** = Hovering over movable piece
- **Game message** = Shows what action to take

### Tips

1. Bring out multiple pieces early for more options
2. Use safe spots strategically
3. Try to capture opponents when possible
4. Plan your moves to get exact rolls for entering home
5. Watch out for opponents near your pieces!

## Troubleshooting

**Can't move pieces?**
- Make sure you've rolled the dice first
- Only pieces with golden rings can be moved
- You need a 6 to bring pieces out from base

**Dice not working?**
- Wait for your turn (check the "Turn" indicator)
- Make sure it's not an AI player's turn
- Try clicking the "Roll Dice" button instead of the dice

**Game stuck?**
- If no valid moves, the game automatically passes to next player
- Rolling a 6 with no moves lets you roll again

## Player Colors

- **Red** (Top-left) - Usually "You" in Quick Play
- **Green** (Top-right)
- **Yellow** (Bottom-right)
- **Blue** (Bottom-left)

Enjoy the game! 🎲
